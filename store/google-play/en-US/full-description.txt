From your first delivery to a growing courier company.

Parcelrise Tycoon is a logistics management game about delivery decisions and running a company. Start with 100 euros and a rented van you drive yourself. Choose your jobs, weigh the costs and invest your earnings in what comes next.

MAKE EVERY DELIVERY COUNT
Set the parcel count, parcel weight and route. Review the expected revenue, expenses and risk before accepting a job. Match each delivery to your vehicle's capacity and your budget, then follow the payouts as jobs finish.

BUILD YOUR FLEET AND TEAM
Buy and sell vehicles, handle repairs and keep up with maintenance. Hire drivers and assign them to vehicles. Run several deliveries at once as your team grows, while balancing vehicle and staff costs.

PLAN YOUR COMPANY'S GROWTH
Work toward company milestones and unlock new regions. Explore hubs and routes on a schematic map inspired by Germany. Follow the income and expense ledger to see how your choices affect the business.

PLAY AT YOUR OWN PACE
Learn your first delivery with the tutorial. Play in English or Turkish without an internet connection. Your progress is saved on your device. A limited amount of elapsed time is processed when you return; new jobs are never accepted automatically.

Will you invest in another vehicle or bring a new driver into your team? Your company's next step is up to you.
